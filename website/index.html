What is SourceMyFuture?

SourceMyFuture offers a comprehensive solution: immersive school programs, storytelling campaigns, mentorship networks and cadetship pathways. Students gain access to LGTA-accredited qualifications and real-world experience through council partnerships, transforming procurement into a career of purpose and innovation.

SourceMyFuture is a values-driven platform committed to repositioning procurement as a strategic and ethical vocation. The platform connects students with education, mentorship, industry leaders and structured career pathways.
